Privacy Policy

Karaoke Search (karaokesearch.uk) · Last updated November 2026

Karaoke Search is designed to work locally in your browser without ads, third-party analytics, or tracking cookies.

1. Using Karaoke Search without an account

You can browse the songbook, save songs and rehearsal notes, and build setlists without signing in. All of this data stays only in your browser's local storage on your device.

2. Optional Google Sign-In & Cloud Sync

If you choose to sign in with Google to sync your songbook across devices:

  • Account identity: We verify your Google ID token and receive only your Google account identifier, display name, and email address. We never see or store passwords.
  • Synced songbook data: We store your saved songs (including status, comfort ratings, preferred keys, and personal notes), current setlist, named saved setlists, and display preferences (theme, compact view, and default singer name) in our Upstash Redis database solely so they sync between your devices.
  • Session cookie: We set a single first-party HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax cookie to keep you signed in. It is not used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

3. Live Multi-Phone Rooms & YouTube Playback

When a host starts a Live Room, the temporary room queue and guest song requests are stored in Upstash Redis and automatically expire after 24 hours (or immediately when the host ends the room). Karaoke videos are embedded from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com).

4. Data Sharing & Deletion

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or saved songbooks with third parties.

You can delete your cloud copy at any time directly inside the app under More tools → Sign in & sync → Delete cloud copy (your local browser copy stays on your device), or request deletion by emailing karaokesearch-support@googlegroups.com.
